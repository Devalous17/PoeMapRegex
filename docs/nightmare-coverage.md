## Conditional thresholds

- Attack block: avoid only for an established attack-hit main skill.
- Flask effect: Pathfinder, Traitor, Wardloop, global effect/charge investment, or confirmed essential flask use. Utility flasks alone do not qualify.
- Extra Chaos: Free with CI/Chaos immunity or at least 70% saved Chaos Resistance; avoid otherwise. 70% is the requested threshold, not a claim of capped resistance.
- Monster crit: Free only with at least 100% saved reduction of extra damage taken from critical strikes; avoid otherwise.
- Cannot Stun: Brick only for an established stun-dependent main setup.

Manual choices still override presets. Missing conditional values fail the requested Chaos/crit allowance, while absence of an established attack/flask/stun dependency does not create one. These policies supersede the earlier automatic Nightmare first-pass ratings. Normal-map rules are unchanged.
